About MyKingdom

Mykingdom is a leading chain of high-end toy stores in Vietnam, specializing in providing high-quality products from famous brands around the world. With the mission of bringing children safe, creative and highly educational toys, Mykingdom has become a trusted destination for parents and children.

At Mykingdom, customers can find thousands of toy products in many different categories such as:

  • Educational toys: LEGO, Siku, K’nex help stimulate creative thinking and train intelligence.
  • Model & assembly toys: Model cars, planes, warships from famous brands such as Takara Tomy, Hot Wheels, and Revell.
  • Doll & cartoon character toys: Barbie, Sylvanian Families, My Little Pony for girls.
  • Remote control toys: Remote control cars, smart robots, mini drones.
  • Sports & exercise toys: Scooters, children's bicycles, sports equipment suitable for many ages.

Store system and online shopping channel

Mykingdom currently has more than 100 stores nationwide, located in major shopping centers and densely populated areas. In addition, customers can easily shop online through Mykingdom's official website or e-commerce platforms such as Shopee, Lazada, Tiki,... with fast and convenient delivery policies.
